How to Play

Extra Turn Rules

If you pocket your own coin, you get another turn! Even if you accidentally pocket an opponent's coin on the same shot, as long as your own coin goes in, you keep your turn.

Queen Rules (Standard)

The Red Queen can be pocketed at any time. However, you must pocket one of your own coins on the same turn to "Cover" the Queen. If you fail, the Queen is returned to the center.

Fouls

Pocketing the Striker is a foul. Your turn ends immediately, and you must return one of your previously pocketed coins to the board.
