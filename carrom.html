How to Play

Directional Play

The board automatically rotates so the active player always shoots from the bottom.

Extra Turn Rules

If you pocket your own assigned coin, you get another turn! (Even if an opponent's coin also falls in).

Queen Cover Rules

If you pocket the Red Queen, you MUST pocket one of your own coins on the very next shot to "Cover" it. If you miss or foul, the Queen goes back to the center!

Fouls & Restrictions

Pocketing the Striker is a foul. Your turn ends and you return one pocketed coin.

Striker Placement: You cannot aim or shoot if the striker overlaps another coin on the baseline (it will glow Red).
