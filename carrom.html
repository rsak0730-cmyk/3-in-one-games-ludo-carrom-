How to Play

Objective

Pocket all 9 of your assigned coins (White for P1, Black for P2).

Controls

Drag the horizontal slider below the board to place your striker on the baseline.
Touch/Drag backward from the striker on the board to aim and shoot.

Queen Rules (Standard)

The Red Queen can be pocketed at any time. However, you must pocket one of your own coins on the same turn to "Cover" the Queen. If you fail, the Queen is returned to the center.

Fouls

Pocketing the Striker is a foul. Your turn ends, and you must return one of your previously pocketed coins to the board (if you have any).
